Memorandum of understanding between the institute and the regency government concerning the development of village information systems and human resource training.
This cooperation agreement is effective from the date of signature and remains valid for three years and may be extended by mutual consent of both parties.
The first party acts for and on behalf of the institute, while the second party acts for and on behalf of the respective cooperation partner.
The scope of cooperation covers education, research, and community service in accordance with the three pillars of higher education.
The person in charge of each activity must submit an implementation report to the cooperation office within two weeks after the activity ends.
Implementation costs are charged to the budget of each party based on the arrangement stated in the implementation agreement.
The dean of the faculty and the head of the study program sign the implementation after receiving approval from the vice rector for partnerships.
Students will take part in industry internships, guest lectures, and joint research projects as part of the independent learning program.
Should a dispute arise, both parties agree to settle it amicably through deliberation before pursuing legal channels.
The quality assurance unit conducts internal quality audits of the cooperation and prepares recommendations for the leadership.
